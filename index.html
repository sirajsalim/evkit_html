Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about WattKit.

Can I compare different electric vehicles?

Yes! WattKit lets you compare two vehicles side-by-side, showing differences in journey costs, battery health projections, winter performance, and more. Export unlimited comparisons as professional PDFs to share with family, friends, or your local EV community.

How are EV journey costs calculated?

WattKit calculates your real journey costs by combining your vehicle's efficiency (miles per kWh), your actual electricity rates for off-peak, standard, and public charging, and factors in seasonal efficiency loss from cold weather. Simply enter the distance you want to travel, and WattKit shows you exactly what it will cost at each charging location.

How much does it cost to charge an electric car at home?

Home charging costs depend on your electricity tariff and region. Many EV owners take advantage of off-peak tariffs to charge overnight at lower rates. WattKit lets you enter your exact off-peak, standard, and public charging rates — in your local currency — so you always know your true costs, not national averages.

How does cold weather affect EV range?

Cold weather can reduce EV range by 15-45% depending on temperature and whether your vehicle has a heat pump. WattKit's winter mode calculates this automatically based on research from the DOE, AAA, and Recurrent Auto (studying 30,000+ vehicles). Select your conditions — mild, typical, or severe winter — and WattKit adjusts your cost calculations accordingly.

How much does EV battery degrade each year?

Battery degradation varies by chemistry. LFP batteries retain about 93% capacity after 5 years, while NMC and NCA batteries retain around 87-88%. WattKit models degradation year-by-year based on real-world studies, showing you projected range and costs over your ownership period. All research sources are linked directly within the app if you want to dive deeper.
