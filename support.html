Getting Started

WattKit helps you understand the real cost of driving an electric vehicle. Here's what you can do:

  • Calculate: Enter your journey details to see real costs — either manually or by selecting a vehicle from the built-in database.
  • Winter Mode: See how cold weather affects your EV's efficiency, with or without a heat pump, across different severity levels.
  • Compare: Put two EVs side by side to compare running costs, and export the results as a PDF.
  • Battery Intelligence: View long-term degradation data by battery chemistry (LFP, NMC, NCA, LMFP) to understand how your battery will age.
  • Widgets: Add Home Screen widgets for at-a-glance cost information in large, medium, or small sizes.
  • Apple Watch: Access key data from your wrist with complications and a dedicated watch app.
